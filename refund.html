Refund Policy

All Net Booster (com.allnetbooster.app). Last updated: 13 November 2026

The app is free. The only thing you can pay for is Pro, a one-time purchase through Google Play that removes all ads.

1. Within 48 hours of buying

Ask Google Play for the refund: open your order history on Google Play, choose the order and tap "Request a refund". Google decides these requests under its own refund rules.

2. After 48 hours, up to 7 days

If Pro does not work for you (for example, ads still appear after buying), write to us within 7 days of the purchase with your Google Play order number (it starts with GPA). We will first try to fix the problem. If we cannot, we refund the full amount.

3. After 7 days

Pro is a one-time purchase and is not refunded after 7 days, unless the law where you live says otherwise.

4. How the money comes back

Refunds go through Google Play to the payment method you used. Google usually completes this in 3 to 5 working days. After a refund, Pro is switched off and the ads return.

5. Bought by mistake, or charged twice

Write to us with the order number. A double charge is always refunded.

6. Contact

Questions: write to hello@locomojo.in.
