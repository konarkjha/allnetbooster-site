Privacy Policy

All Net Booster (com.allnetbooster.app). Last updated: 13 November 2026

Short version: the app has no account and no server of its own. Your internet traffic is never sent to us. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects some data to do that. Pro has no ads.

1. What we collect

Nothing. The developer of All Net Booster runs no server for this app, and the app sends no information about you, your phone, your traffic or your downloads to the developer.

2. The VPN permission

All Net Booster uses Android's VPN permission (VpnService) for one purpose: to see each connection that apps on your phone open, and to send it out through Wi-Fi, mobile data or ethernet. This is the core function of the app.

  • It is a local VPN. Your traffic goes directly from your own networks to the internet. It does not pass through any server of ours or of anyone we work with.
  • The app does not read, record, change or sell the contents of your traffic.
  • The app does not block or filter ads in other apps through the VPN.
  • To choose a network, the app keeps the address of each open connection in memory while the booster is on. This is not written to storage and not sent anywhere.

3. What stays on your phone

  • Your settings, the list of apps you chose to leave out, and how much data each network used today.
  • The list of your downloads (address, file name, size) and the files themselves, in your Downloads folder.
  • Cookies and site data of pages you open in the download browser, as in any browser. The app keeps no browsing history.
  • If you bought Pro: the purchase reference Google Play gave the app.

All of this is removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app. Downloaded files stay until you delete them.

4. Ads in the free version (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads supplied by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google's software in the app collects and uses data such as your phone's advertising ID, IP address (which gives an approximate location), device model and system version, and how you interact with the ads. Google is responsible for that data under its own policy: How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or turn off ad personalisation, in your phone's Settings under Google, Ads.
  • Where the law requires it (for example in Europe and the UK), the app asks for your choice before any ad is requested. You can change that choice later in Settings, under Ad privacy choices.
  • With Pro, the app does not load ads.

5. Payments

Pro is bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment. The app and its developer never see your card, UPI or bank details.

6. Share with PC

When you turn this on, a computer on the same Wi-Fi or tethered to your phone can send its traffic through your phone. This works only on your local network and only while the booster is on.

7. Permissions the app asks for

  • VPN: explained in section 2.
  • Network state and changing it: to see which networks you have and to keep mobile data on next to Wi-Fi.
  • Notifications and running in the foreground: to keep the booster and your downloads running and show their status.
  • Install apps: asked only when you tap Install on an APK you downloaded.
  • The list of apps on your phone that can be opened: shown so you can leave some apps out of the booster. It never leaves the phone.
  • Advertising ID: used by Google AdMob, see section 4.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any data from them.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published here with a new date. If the app ever starts to use a server (for example a future mode that joins your networks through a server), this policy will be updated before that feature is released.
