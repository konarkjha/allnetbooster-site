About All Net Booster

Version 1.0.0

All Net Booster lets your phone use Wi-Fi, mobile data and USB-C ethernet at the same time, instead of only one of them.

What it does

  • Boost. Each new connection your apps open goes out through one of your networks in turn, so the load is spread over all of them.
  • Turbo Download. One file is downloaded in pieces over all your networks at once and joined on the phone.
  • Download browser. A browser inside the app that catches download links and blocks ads on the pages you open in it.
  • Share with PC. A computer on the same Wi-Fi or tethered to the phone can use the combined connection.

What it cannot do

There is no server behind this app. One single connection still travels over one network, so an app that uses only one connection (many video and store apps) does not get faster. Downloads made with Turbo Download and apps that open several connections do.

Mobile data used by the app counts against your data plan. You can set a daily limit in Settings.

Free and Pro

The app is free and shows a banner ad at the bottom of the screen, and now and then a full-screen ad just after you turn Boost on or start a download (never more than one in three minutes). Pro is a one-time purchase through Google Play that removes all ads.
